# Eurostat Statistical Monitor: Household Financial Mobility in France, Germany & Spain

**Source:** Eurostat Banking Statistics Division  
**Data Cycle:** 2024–2025 Retrospective  

## Comparative Findings Across Core European Jurisdictions

- **Germany:** High inter-bank mobility due to dense fintech and direct-bank alternatives (e.g. DKB, ING-DiBa, N26, Sparkassen). Depositors proactively move deposits when interest yield spreads widen by as little as 25 basis points.
- **France:** High relationship longevity anchored by "Livre A" regulated tax-sheltered savings accounts and primary mortgage domicile laws. Retail customers exhibit high baseline inertia unless experiencing severe service failure.
- **Spain:** Post-consolidation stabilization with dominant nationwide networks (Santander, BBVA, CaixaBank). Account closures correlate primarily with employment shifts and demographic retirement cycles rather than yield hunting.
- **Age Dynamics:** Across all three nations, customers aged 46–60 represent the peak churn cohort, driven by retirement transitions, pension consolidations, and inheritance disbursements.
