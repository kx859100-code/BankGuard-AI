# ECB Financial Stability Review: Retail Deposit Stickiness & Multi-Product Attrition

**Source:** European Central Bank (ECB) Analytical Bulletin  
**Classification:** Regulatory & Empirical Research  
**Topic:** Household Deposit Elasticity, Yield-Sensitivity, and Cross-Border Banking Churn  

## Executive Summary
Recent monetary tightening cycles across the Eurozone have highlighted asymmetric deposit elasticity across retail customer categories. In particular:

1. **High-Balance Deposit Sensitivity:** Retail customers with liquid balances exceeding €100,000 exhibit a 2.4x higher switching propensity in response to cross-institution yield differentials compared to mass-market depositors.
2. **Account Inactivity as a Lead Indicator:** Operational data confirms that customer accounts exhibiting zero login activity over a rolling 90-day window face a 68% probability of relationship termination within the subsequent 12 months.
3. **Cross-Border Divergence:** In Germany, the presence of a decentralized retail banking system (comprising savings banks, cooperative lenders, and private commercial institutions) creates significantly lower switching friction. Consequently, German retail churn rates consistently benchmark higher (~30–33%) relative to France (~15–18%) and Spain (~16–19%), where institutional concentration is higher.
