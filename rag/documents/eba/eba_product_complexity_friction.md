# EBA Supervisory Guidelines: Product Bundling Friction & Retail Attrition

**Source:** European Banking Authority (EBA)  
**Topic:** Retail Consumer Outcomes, Multi-Product Bundles, and Contractual Churn  

## Key Regulatory Findings

1. **Multi-Product Complexity Trap:** While cross-selling two primary products (e.g. current checking account + residential mortgage or debit facility) produces maximum customer lifetime stickiness (churn < 10%), holding 3 or 4 disparate financial products correlates with an acute spike in customer attrition (exceeding 75%).
2. **Root Causes of Friction:** Customer complaints filed across National Competent Authorities (NCAs) indicate that holding 3+ unbundled products generates compound maintenance fees, fragmented customer support lines, and conflicting transactional statements.
3. **Supervisory Recommendation:** Financial institutions observing heightened churn among multi-product account holders must establish structured retention interventions centered on **Product Consolidation, Fee Harmonization, and Unified Single-Point-of-Contact Relationship Management**.
